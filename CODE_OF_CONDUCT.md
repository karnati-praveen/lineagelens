# Code of Conduct

LineageLens is a small open source project. We want it to be a welcoming place for contributors.

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good intent. Focus on the work, not the person.

## What we ask

- Be kind and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions
- Welcome contributors at all experience levels
- Accept feedback graciously — disagreeing is fine; being dismissive is not
- Keep discussions on-topic

## What we won't tolerate

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or the project

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, email **karnatipraveen17@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.

Violations may result in comments being removed, or contributors being blocked from the project.
